# Haq business plan (made with cfo.ai)

These are exports from cfo.ai. The forecast runs for 24 months, from Nov 2026 (month 1) to Oct 2028 (month 24), and covers three scenarios: Main, Conservative and Growth. A summary is in the [README](../../README.md#business-plan-made-with-cfoai).

- `assumptions.xlsx`: every input of the Main scenario, with its value, unit, basis (MEASURED / ASSUMED) and a short explanation.
- `monthly-plan.xlsx`: the Main scenario's month-by-month P&L and cash flow for all 24 months, covering customers, checks, revenue, AI cost, payroll and ending cash.
- `economics.xlsx`: unit economics per check for each customer type (CSC, CSR, employer) at ₹20, ₹10 and ₹4 AI cost; the scenario decisions; and the break-even sensitivity matrix (AI cost per check × CSC operators at month 12).
- `calculation-checks.xlsx`: the monthly decision checks (future minimum profit, acquisition top-up) and the break-even sensitivity behind the economics sheet.
- `forecast-dashboard.xlsx`: a side-by-side comparison of the three scenarios (break-even, minimum cash, funding needed, acquisition spend), plus the model inputs for all three.

Some cells hold values that cfo.ai calculated rather than live formulas, so they do not recalculate. Each workbook's `Export notes` sheet lists them.
